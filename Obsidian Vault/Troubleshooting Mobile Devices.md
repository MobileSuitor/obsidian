- **General Steps**
	1. Close, uninstall, and reinstall an app
	2. Restart the device
	3. Reboot the device
	4. Update, repair, or reinstall the OS, or recover the system from the last backup
		- backup content and settings first
	5. Start over by resetting the device to its factory state
- **Common Problems and Solutions**
	- *Short battery life or power drain* - disable wireless connections and close apps when not in use. Consider the possibility of malware. Try different charger cables or exchanging the battery unless device is under warranty
	- *Screen does not autorotate* - go to Settings > Display & Touch > verify auto-rotate is on
	- *OS fails to update* - check amount of free space on device and uninstall stuff if you need more
	- *Apps fail to launch/slow to respond* - may be due to hot or failing battery. Close apps not in use, clean cached data, disable live wallpapers. Try to update or reinstall apps, uninstall unused apps, consider factory reset
	- *Random reboots* - usually caused by bad apps, overheating, corrupted OS, or defective hardware. Uninstall suspected apps and update OS
